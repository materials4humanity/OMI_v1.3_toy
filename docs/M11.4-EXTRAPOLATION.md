# M11.4 — the extrapolation experiment, Generator A

**Result: NO BENEFIT on the pre-registered criterion. Generator B was not run.**

**And the null is uninformative, for a reason that is itself the milestone's main
finding.** The experiment as designed could not have detected the effect it was
built to test, and diagnosing why produced a defect in Spec §9.3 (`E-39`).

Thresholds fixed at `bd44bb6`, before the sweep script existed
(`docs/M11.4-PREREGISTRATION.md`). Design: ADR-045 as amended.
Reproduce: `python scripts/run_m11_4_extrapolation.py`.

---

## 1. The pre-registered result

Held-out strain rates `[20, 200]`, outside `KOCKS_MECKING`'s declared window
`[1e-3, 10]` — extrapolation factor **min 3.02, median 11.88, max 38.34**, so every
evaluation point is genuinely outside the declared range.

| Contestant | held-out RMSE | sd |
|---|---|---|
| 1 free-form | 7.31512 | 0.155 |
| 2 correct form | 7.33298 | 0.202 |
| 3a missing mechanism | 7.52630 | 0.199 |
| 3b missing dependence | 7.21154 | 0.122 |
| 4 Ridge | 7.32512 | 0.158 |
| 4 GBT | 7.25594 | 0.146 |

| Comparison | gap | τ (pre-registered) | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1 − 3a** | −0.211 | 0.389 | **no effect** |
| **1 − 3b** | +0.104 | 0.367 | **no effect** |
| 1 − 2 (ceiling) | −0.018 | 0.352 | no effect |
| 1 − 4 Ridge | −0.010 | 0.352 | no effect |
| 1 − 4 GBT | +0.059 | 0.353 | no effect |

**The claim — `gap(3, 1)` and `gap(3, 4)` — is not supported.** Neither
misspecification arm beats the free-form incumbent or the tabular baselines by more
than its pre-registered threshold. Per ADR-045 and the amendment, **Generator B was
not run**: a form that cannot beat free-form when the only withheld physics is one
named term will not do better when an entire coupling is missing.

**Contestant 2 carries ADR-045's caveat and is reported as a ceiling, not a
headline.** Its gap is −0.018: with a *correct* form and fitted parameters it is
indistinguishable from free-form on this criterion. That is not the strong-prior win
the ADR anticipated, and it is the first sign that the design, not the hypothesis, is
what failed here.

**3a versus 3b did not diverge at the tested rollout length**: +0.315 against a
declared minimum effect of 0.702, so "comparable". The question you asked — whether
the benefit depends on *which kind* of misspecification the declared physics carries
— is therefore **not answered at unit strain**. See §3.

## 2. Why the null is uninformative — the design failed, not just the hypothesis

Every contestant's held-out RMSE lies in `[7.21, 7.53]`, a spread of **0.31**. The
withheld drag term's own magnitude over the held-out region is **7.18**. So every
contestant's entire held-out error *is* the withheld term, to within the spread
between them.

The diagnostic that settles it — error against a **drag-free** generator, measuring
how well each contestant captures everything *except* the withheld term:

| Contestant | vs full truth | **vs drag-free** |
|---|---|---|
| 1 free-form | 7.52 | **0.077** |
| 2 correct form | 7.60 | 0.339 |
| 3a missing mechanism | 7.84 | 2.494 |
| 3b missing dependence | 7.32 | 1.774 |
| 4 Ridge | 7.63 | 0.364 |
| 4 GBT | 7.40 | 0.351 |

**The free-form arm is the *best* of all six against the drag-free truth**, four
times better than the correct declared form. That is not a fluke and it is the whole
diagnosis: **Kocks–Mecking has no strain-rate dependence.** The drag-free physics is
therefore *constant* along the held-out axis, and a free-form surface that learned
"output does not depend on rate" in-envelope extrapolates that flat function
perfectly. The declared form's structural knowledge — `√ρ` storage, `ρ` recovery —
buys nothing along an axis it says nothing about.

So the held-out axis was chosen such that:

1. the declared form makes **no prediction that differs** from a constant, and
2. the only variation along it is the **withheld term**, which no contestant has.

Every contestant is wrong by the same amount, for the same reason, and the
comparison cannot discriminate. **The null is real and correctly measured; it is
simply not evidence about the hypothesis.**

This is my design error, and it was not caught by the pre-registration because the
pre-registration checked that the thresholds were sane, not that the hold-out was
*informative*. That gap is now `E-39`.

## 3. A pre-specified observation that generates an untested hypothesis

ADR-045 required rollout-length error curves for every contestant (CLAUDE.md §5
invariant 7), so this table is a **pre-specified output**, not a post-hoc search:

| Contestant | γ=0.25 | 0.5 | **1.0** | 2.0 | 4.0 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 free-form | 1.21 | 2.11 | **7.32** | 18.33 | **40.91** |
| 2 correct form | 1.91 | 3.75 | **7.33** | 13.41 | **20.11** |
| 3a missing mechanism | 1.67 | 3.38 | **7.53** | 19.44 | **57.52** |
| 3b missing dependence | 1.91 | 3.73 | **7.21** | 12.87 | **18.49** |
| 4 Ridge | 1.21 | 2.11 | **7.33** | 18.35 | **40.95** |
| 4 GBT | 1.23 | 2.09 | **7.26** | 18.21 | **40.68** |

At the tested rollout length the contestants are indistinguishable. At γ=4 the two
declared-form arms that retain a recovery term (2 and 3b) sit at **20.1 and 18.5**
while free-form and both tabular baselines sit at **40.7–41.0** — roughly a **2×**
separation. And 3a, which dropped recovery entirely, is **worst of all** at 57.5.

**This is consistent with the diagnosis in §2 and must not be read as a result.**
Along the *strain* axis the declared form does make a differing prediction — recovery
produces saturation, which a quadratic surface extrapolates as unbounded growth — so
the benefit appears exactly where the declared form has content, and vanishes on the
axis where it does not. That is the pattern the hypothesis predicts.

**It is nonetheless untested.** The thresholds were registered for held-out RMSE at
unit strain; applying them to a γ=4 gap would be moving the goalposts after seeing
the numbers, which is what the pre-registration exists to prevent. Testing it
requires a **fresh pre-registration** with the rollout length declared in advance and
a hold-out chosen so the declared form and the baseline differ along the held-out
axis. That is a follow-on experiment, and this document does not claim its result.

Note also that 3a and 3b diverge sharply at γ=4 (57.5 versus 18.5) while being
comparable at γ=1. If that survives a proper test, it answers the question of whether
the benefit depends on the *kind* of misspecification — and answers it "yes, and by a
wide margin." Untested, for the same reason.

## 4. What is and is not claimed

**Claimed.** On Generator A, with a held-out control-space region and thresholds
pre-registered before the sweep, declared constitutive forms show **no benefit** over
a free-form operator or unchanged tabular baselines at unit strain. Generator B was
not run.

**Not claimed.** That declared physics buys no reach. This experiment could not have
detected it, for the reason in §2.

**Recorded as a defect in the framework, not only in this build.** Spec §9.3 requires
that "extrapolation tests hold out entire regions of the control space" and says
nothing about the held-out region needing to be one where the candidate and the
baseline actually differ. This experiment satisfied §9.3 in full and produced a
vacuous comparison — `docs/V1.4-EDITS.md` **E-39**.

**Standing limitation from the pre-registration**, repeated as promised: the
standard errors were measured in-envelope, which may understate held-out
variability and therefore makes τ tighter and a positive finding *easier*. Since the
finding is negative, that limitation cuts against the null being an artefact of an
over-loose threshold — the thresholds were, if anything, too easy to clear, and
nothing cleared them.
