## 1. §5.2a — The E-48 triage

### Question 1: contrast's construction, or the statistic itself?

**Neither, as the question was framed.** It is a property of the share's **value set**,
which is discrete for structural reasons on *every* chain in this repository — and the
domains sit at **opposite** extremes.

| chain | near-diagonal share values | smallest margin to `0.5` |
|---|---|---|
| contrast (empty erasure inventory) | exact rational ladder `1/(D−k)`: `1/12, 1/10, 1/8, 1/6, 1/4, 1/2` | **`< 10⁻⁹`** — it lands *on* the threshold |
| flagship, declared chain | exactly `0.0` or `1.0` | **`0.5`** |
| flagship, same operators composed to contrast's length | `≈ 0.99995` at every index | `> 0.4` |
| flagship_constitutive (M11's chain) | exactly `0.0` or `1.0` | **`0.5`** |

**There is no natural discontinuity at `0.5`.** ADR-020's threshold is arbitrary. The
statistic behaves as a *boolean* on flagship and as a *rational ladder* on contrast; neither
has any structural feature at the threshold. Contrast lands on it because its ladder passes
through it — at `k = D − 2`, whatever the numbers are — and flagship never approaches it.

**But contrast's landing is not luck either, and the mechanism is measured.** At the index
where the label flips, three observations are relevant:

| observation | contribution to the direction's Gramian quadratic form | share of total |
|---|---|---|
| `j = k` (at the index) | **`0.0`** | `0.000000000000` |
| `j = k+1` | `2.251302×10²` | `0.500000000060` |
| `j = k+2` | `2.251302×10²` | `0.499999999940` |

The two contributing terms are **identical to eleven significant figures**. So the
near-diagonal window catches exactly one of two equal halves and the share **is** the
threshold — not near it. Which side of ADR-020's strict `> 0.5` a direction falls on is
decided by the last bits of two mathematically equal floats.

This happens whenever the chain's Jacobian is **state-independent and non-contracting** on
the observed components: every downstream observation then contributes the same quadratic
form, and the share collapses to a **ratio of term counts** — a rational with a small
denominator. `predicted_contrast_share(k) = 1/(D−k)` is derived from the chain's structure
before any Gramian is formed and matches the measurement to `< 10⁻⁹`.

**Which extreme a chain lands at is predicted by one declared property: whether it declares
an erasure operator.** Flagship and flagship_constitutive both do (`is_erasure` is `True` on
their first segment) and sit at the boolean extreme; contrast declares an empty inventory and
sits on the ladder. That is precisely the axis Core §7.2 chose the two domains to invert — so
the degeneracy is **generic for Core §3.9's condition-(b) class**, erasure-free chains that
must control error by observation density instead, and not a quirk of one instantiation.

**A third leg the question did not ask about.** The threshold is not the only undeclared
quantity deciding the label. ADR-020's `near_diagonal_window` operationalises Spec §3.3's
"`j ≈ k`", which Spec also leaves unspecified. Same chain, same index, same two directions:

| window | share | labels |
|---|---|---|
| `0` | `0.0` | both **inferred** |
| `1` | `0.5000000000596` / `0.4999999999856` | **split** |
| `2` | `1.0` | both **observed** |

The window spans the entire range of answers on its own.

### Question 2: is a threshold on a continuous share the right form?

**No — and the structural alternative is Spec's own sentence, which ADR-020 does not
implement.** Spec §3.3, verbatim:

> *"a direction is **observed** if a **single** near-diagonal term (`j ≈ k`) **dominates**,
> and **inferred** if information accrues **only** through `Σ_{j>k}`"*

Two departures, neither recorded as one:

| Spec says | ADR-020 implements |
|---|---|
| a **single** near-diagonal term dominates | the **sum** of all near-diagonal terms in a window exceeds half the total |
| inferred iff information accrues **only** downstream | inferred iff the share is **not above** the threshold |

The first replaces a statement about one term with one about an aggregate — and on contrast
the aggregate is carried entirely by `j = k+1` while the term *at* the index contributes
`0.0`, so no single near-diagonal term dominates anything. The second replaces a **support**
condition (contribution is zero) with a **magnitude** condition, labelling a direction that
gets 49% of its information near-diagonally as though it got none.

**And Spec's two conditions are not complementary.** Between "one term dominates" and "the
near-diagonal contribution is zero" lies every case where near-diagonal terms contribute
something but no single one dominates — which is where contrast sits at every interval. Under
Spec's own words that direction is **unlabelled**, and Spec offers no third label. So the
threshold an implementation must invent is filling a hole in the **definition**, not merely
an unspecified number. Filed as **E-53**.

**Three structural alternatives, stated as a fork because they disagree on real cases:**

| # | Form | Needs a new number? | On contrast's flip index |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Support test** — `observed` iff `vᵀ𝐆_near v > 0` at ADR-017's declared rank tolerance | **No** | both `observed` (share `0.5 > 0`) |
| 2 | **Single-term dominance** — `observed` iff `max_{j near} q_j > ρ · max_{j>k} q_j` | one ratio `ρ ≥ 1` | **neither** (ratio exactly `1`) |
| 3 | **Abstention band** — keep the share, refuse to label within a declared band | one band | neither; reports the tie |

Option 1 is Spec's *"only"* made exact and is cheapest, but it makes "dominates" vacuous —
any nonzero near-diagonal contribution earns `observed`. Option 2 is Spec's *"a single term
dominates"* made exact, and its number is a **ratio between comparable quantities** rather
than a fraction of a total, so it degrades gracefully where the share does not: contrast's
two equal terms give ratio exactly `1`, which fails any `ρ > 1` cleanly and reports the tie
instead of resolving it by rounding. Option 3 is the framework's own refusal discipline —
Core §3.9, CLAUDE.md §4 — converting an arbitrary label into an honest abstention.

Option 1 inherits the window dependence unchanged; option 2 largely escapes it, since a
single-term maximum is insensitive to how many terms the window admits; option 3 escapes
none of it.

**E-53's proposed wording implements option 2 with option 3's abstention**, and carries
`[authorial-choice]` on the selection, because the three change which directions a
practitioner is told the chain model is carrying — Core §3.8's "distinctive contribution".
That is a decision about what the framework claims, not an implementation detail. **Nothing
is fixed in this part.**

### Consequence for the domain designed next

An SDL/discovery domain deciding what to make is a **composition inverse run repeatedly**,
and its natural chain has a strong erasure (calcination). So it lands at flagship's extreme
rather than contrast's — the observed/inferred label will be near-trivially `observed`, which
is degenerate in the opposite direction and equally uninformative. The declaration records
that prediction rather than leaving Part 6 to meet it.

## 2. §5(2) — The discovery domain's declaration

`src/omi_domains/sdl/` — `state.py`, `forms.py`, `interface.py`. **No `EvolutionOperator`
and no campaign.**

### Why it is a third decision kind

| Domain | Control axis | Decision | Inverse |
|---|---|---|---|
| flagship | **apparatus**-determined | which route for a given material | process inverse |
| contrast | **usage**-determined | how to operate a given artefact | usage inverse |
| **discovery** | **acquisition**-determined | **what to make** | **composition inverse** |

An apparatus operator follows a route; a service duty cycle is imposed by the application; an
acquisition policy chooses *the next thing to exist*. That moves the Parameter-role quantities
out of the fixed index into the decision variables, which is ADR-053's definition of the
composition inverse — so composition is the primary designed variable, which no existing
domain exercises.

### Machine-diffable, and different on every item

`diff(SDL_V13_CORE, FLAGSHIP_DECLARATION)` and `diff(SDL_V13_CORE, CONTRAST_DECLARATION)`
both report **all seven items differing**, plus `invariants_structural`. Comparability is
preserved by construction: the v1.3 seven items sit whole inside the v1.4 carrier, which sits
whole inside the v1.5 carrier (ADR-042 applied twice; ADR-059). `omi.interface.diff` still
receives a v1.3 object, so every published comparability result survives unchanged.

`SpecificationVersion` gains `PROPOSED_V1_5`, as a read-only property — E-35's argument that
a level name needs its version does not stop applying at the second extension.

### The seven items, in brief

| Item | Declared |
|---|---|
| 1 state | `m`: loading, particle size. `z`: active-site density, defect-site fraction. `ν`: pore-network accessibility. `Γ`: support-interface coverage, reconstruction index |
| 2 control | **acquisition-determined** recipe + evaluation condition; `𝒰_adm` bounded by rig limits *and by the attainable region* |
| 3 erasure | `CALCINATION` — non-empty, unlike contrast |
| 4 readouts | turnover frequency (0/A), selectivity (0/A), deactivation onset (0/**B**) |
| 5 observations | four modalities, all four backed by state components — unlike contrast's declared-but-unevaluable third |
| 6 invariants | one conservation, two monotonicity |
| 7 scale | two-tier; closure defect declared **unmeasured**, not assumed small (Spec §6 is an anti-goal) |

**Composition is deliberately not in the schema.** It indexes the operator family and is
transported by nothing — ADR-051's Parameter role, realised as ADR-049's `INVARIANT`
coupling on item 1's refinement. Putting it in a slot would type a fixed index as a state,
the mis-typing E-46 separates.

### The v1.5 refinements, each exercised rather than asserted

**Species roles per region (ADR-051), with the central claim exercised.** The promoter is
`CONTROL` in the bulk (chosen per sample) and `STATE` in the surface layer (it segregates
during calcination and evolves under that operator) — **simultaneously, in one chain.** This
is the first declaration in the repository where ADR-051's claim is exhibited rather than
argued. Support is `PARAMETER` in both regions, so `parameter_only_species()` returns
`('support',)` — E-29's part 2 made mechanical.

**Coupled quantities (ADR-049, ADR-050, ADR-052).** Three, one per coupling direction that
matters here:

| quantity | domain | coupling | why |
|---|---|---|---|
| `mean_composition` | `REGIONS` | `INVARIANT` | constant over the **closed bulk** |
| `pore_network_accessibility` | `GLOBAL_POINT` | `DETERMINED_BY` | no local value even in principle — E-22's revised case, on a fourth independent domain |
| `surface_promoter_enrichment` | `REGIONS` | `DEPLETED_BY` | promoter arriving at the surface *leaves* the bulk |

The closure note on `mean_composition` states in advance that the surface layer is **not**
closed, so ADR-050's constancy check is **expected to fail** there and the failure means the
coupling should have been `DEPLETED_BY` — which the third quantity declares explicitly. The
pair therefore *exhibits* ADR-050's corrective diagnosis rather than merely permitting it.

**Descriptor metric (ADR-052).** Three descriptors declared **with** the underlying fractions;
a declaration carrying descriptors alone is refused at construction, because a descriptor
without its ground truth cannot be held out against.

**Tracked dimensions with justification (ADR-049).** Non-empty at all three quantities and
enforced at construction. Stated honestly in the module docstring and here: the free-text
field **checks nothing by itself** — what it buys is visibility to `interface.diff`. The
enumerated reason code remains a live candidate needing its own decision and is **not**
implemented.

**Composition-dependent validity (ADR-054).** `COMPOSITION_VALIDITY_INTERVAL` declares the
descriptor interval over which the two forms' validity ranges are *themselves* claimed to
hold — the second-order declaration. Its **refusal** outside that interval is declared and
not implemented, because the refusal belongs to the operator that consumes the form and this
part declares no operator.

### The attainability region — declared, not deferred

ADR-053 designed the composition inverse and **declined** to design its infeasibility
certificate, because it needs a declared attainable region and none existed. This domain
declares one:

- per-descriptor bounds (3);
- per-coordinate underlying bounds (4);
- a **simplex** constraint — architecture, not a penalty (invariant 5): a normalisation
  violated by 3% is not a slightly wrong composition, it is not a composition;
- one **pairwise exclusion** with its mechanism *and its budget*;
- a prose **route note**, marked as the one constraint here that is not machine-checkable.

`AttainableRegion.report` returns four distinct verdicts, all measured:

| request | verdict | binding |
|---|---|---|
| interior formulation | `ATTAINABLE` | `none` |
| valence electron count `14.0` | `OUTSIDE_DESCRIPTOR_BOUNDS` | `descriptor:valence_electron_count` |
| fractions summing to `1.07` | `UNDERLYING_INFEASIBLE` | `underlying:sums_to_one` |
| promoter `0.07` with metal_b `0.25` | `EXCLUDED_PAIR` | `excluded:promoter+metal_b` |

A missing constraint value **raises** rather than being skipped — a constraint silently
omitted from a certificate is one whose violation cannot be seen, and it is the rule
ADR-043's `ValidityRange.report` already applies.

That the certificate names *which* constraint bound is what makes the composition inverse a
third inverse problem by Core §5's own separating criterion, rather than a relabelled
structure inverse.

**One design correction worth recording.** The exclusion's product budget was first a module
constant in `omi/proposed/v15.py`, and the first realistic interior formulation was certified
`EXCLUDED_PAIR` by it. The fix was **not** to retune the constant — that would be adjusting a
criterion to make a case fit. It was that the constant was in the wrong place: "cannot both be
high" is a claim about a particular chemistry, so a fixed budget in domain-neutral code is a
domain claim on the wrong side of invariant 3, and a number no domain ever had to defend
(E-44's shape). The budget is now domain-declared with its basis stated, and an exclusion with
a non-positive budget or an empty reason is refused at construction.

### Which diagnostics this domain makes live that contrast did not

| Diagnostic | On contrast | On discovery |
|---|---|---|
| **Validity report** (Spec §2.2 / ADR-043) | **dark** — no declared form, nothing to report against | **live** — two forms, chain-level worst case aggregates, `ValidityAction` can separate *re-run the control inverse* from *buy physics* |
| **Blocking trichotomy** (Spec §1.7) | live | live |
| **Innovation sequence** (Spec §10) | live as a gate, not via `any_drift` (E-49) | same; E-49 is unaffected by the domain |
| **Danger score / triage** (Spec §3.3) | degenerate on the ladder; fires arbitrarily | **still degenerate, at the other extreme** |

**Exactly one becomes live, and the honest accounting of the other three matters more.**

- The **triage's influence-median degeneracy is not escaped**: three target readouts over a
  seven-component state means more than half the eigendirections again have exactly zero
  influence, so the median split is again a tautology. Nothing in this declaration changes
  that, and nothing in it should — E-48's fix is a framework decision, and pre-empting it in a
  domain declaration would hide the defect behind a domain choice.
- The **observed/inferred split moves extreme rather than being fixed**: declaring an erasure
  puts this chain at flagship's boolean end, so the label will be near-trivially `observed`.
  Asserted now, so Part 6 cannot mistake a trivial `observed` for an informative one.
- The validity report, now live, **still cannot say whether the mechanism set is complete for
  the regime** — M11.5's measured finding, and the reason §11's buy-physics row reads
  "signalled on the wrong axis". Declaring forms makes the diagnostic live; it does not make it
  sufficient.

## 3. What Part 6 inherits

- A discriminating statistic already selected (`innovation_bias`, Part 5(1)), and E-50's
  finding that the vacuity gate it must pass needs a fourth outcome first.
- A domain whose composition inverse has a certificate, and whose validity report is live.
- Two diagnostics known degenerate in advance, with the degeneracies characterised well
  enough to predict which extreme this domain shows.
- E-53's fork **unresolved**, deliberately: the observed/inferred form is an authorial
  decision, and Part 6 must not settle it by picking whichever option makes its numbers
  cleaner.
