# V1.5-PART5-1.md — the contrast decision loop, the statistic dry-run, and E-47 measured

**Live document** (CLAUDE.md §10): no commit stamp, corrected in place. Every number
below is regenerated by `python scripts/run_v15_part5_1.py` and asserted by
`tests/oracles/test_contrast_diagnostic_trace.py`,
`tests/oracles/test_statistic_dry_run.py` and `tests/oracles/test_parameter_ridge.py`.

**Scope.** v1.5 planning Part 5(1). The decision loop is **design and ADRs only** —
ADR-056 — and no loop is implemented. What executes is the loop's *inputs*, the statistic
dry-run, and the E-47 measurement. Part 5(2) (the third decision kind) and Part 6 (the
experiment) are not begun. **No threshold is fixed here**; Part 6's pre-registration owns
them, in its own earlier commit.

---

## 1. The headline

Three results, and the first two are negative in a way that is worth more than a working
demonstration would have been.

1. **Of the four diagnostics the decision loop was to be driven by, three cannot drive it
   on contrast** — and for three *different* reasons, none of which is a property of the
   loop. One is structurally unavailable, one is degenerate, and one is calibrated at the
   window level and consumed at the campaign level with a 38.5% false-alarm rate.
2. **The categorical triage does not merely fail to fire; it fires arbitrarily.** Two
   eigendirections whose near-diagonal shares agree to eleven decimal places receive
   different labels, because Spec §3.3's observed/inferred distinction is operationalised
   as a strict inequality with no declared margin.
3. **E-47 measures, and is narrowed by measuring.** The parameter ridge is real
   (condition number 436.7) and rotates toward the extrapolation-sensitive direction
   (5.5×). It never aligns with it (|cos| 0.240 at 6× the envelope), and the declared
   validity report is *not* silent — it fires for 41.7% of far queries, for the wrong
   reason.

Five ledger entries follow: **E-48** through **E-52**.

## 2. §5.1 — Which diagnostics fired, and which never did

ADR-056 designs an **ordered** rule — retire, then commission, then derate, then set
control at nominal — because Spec §7.3 already requires an ordered infeasibility
diagnosis and E-06's finding is that an unordered report cannot say which of several
terms is responsible. The ordering is also what makes the vacuity ablation
interpretable: with four terms in one weighted score, freezing one changes the sum a
little and the argmax rarely, so "did this diagnostic drive anything" stops being
answerable.

**The loop is not implemented.** What was measured is the trace of its inputs over a
24-interval declared contrast campaign with per-interval voltage telemetry, so ADR-056's
predicates could be evaluated against recorded readings.

### The verdict per diagnostic

| Diagnostic | Can it drive a decision? | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Validity report** (Spec §2.2 / ADR-043) | **No — structurally unavailable** | contrast declares no constitutive form, so its operator does not satisfy `ConstitutivelyConstrained` and there is nothing to report against |
| **Danger score / triage** (Spec §3.3) | **No — and worse: it fires arbitrarily** | influence median exactly 0.0 at every interval; total danger flat to 6×10⁻⁵ of its own mean; the label split decided at a margin of 1.4×10⁻¹¹ |
| **Innovation sequence** (Spec §10) | **Yes, as a gate — but not via `any_drift`** | per-window rate nominal (0.0561 vs 0.05, mean NIS 1.0074); `any_drift` fires on 38.5% of *correctly specified* campaigns |
| **Blocking trichotomy** (Spec §1.7) | **Yes** | continuous, and the only one of the four that ranks a *purchase* |

### The triage's two degeneracies

**The influence boundary collapses the classification.** Influence is the quadratic form
`vᵀSᵀWSv` and is non-negative. `dendrite_risk` depends on two of seven state components,
so more than half the eigendirections have influence exactly zero, the median *is* zero,
and `influence ≥ influence_median` is a tautology. Every direction is "influential";
`MARGINALISABLE` and `OBSERVED_BUT_IRRELEVANT` are unreachable; Spec §3.3's four-way
triage silently becomes the two-way identifiability split. This is not a contrast
property — it holds for any domain whose target set touches fewer than half the state
components.

`tests/test_domain_triage.py` already recorded `contrast_influence_median == 0.0` at
index 0 and asserted it. What is new is the consequence.

**The near-diagonal boundary decides the rest at machine precision.** Over the same
campaign the share passes smoothly through 0.5. At the interval where it does:

| direction | near-diagonal share | label |
|---|---|---|
| A | `0.5000000000596156` | **observed** |
| B | `0.4999999999856102` | **inferred** |

The two agree to eleven decimal places. The distinction being decided is Core §3.8's own
— whether "the chain model, not any instrument, is doing the work". Smallest margin to
the threshold anywhere in the campaign: `1.4×10⁻¹¹`.

**So the failure is not silence.** The label set changes at two of thirteen sampled
intervals, so a rule keyed on labels *does* fire — at intervals distinguished by nothing
physical. An arbitrarily firing diagnostic is worse than a silent one, and it is why
ADR-056's step 2 is keyed on the blocking trichotomy and `ΔV_c/cost` rather than on
`dangerous_set()`. Filed as **E-48**.

### The drift monitor: calibrated, then aggregated badly

Measured over 200 **correctly specified** campaigns — the model that generated the data
is the model the filter runs, so every flag is a false alarm by construction:

| quantity | measured | expected |
|---|---|---|
| mean NIS | `1.0074` | `1.0` |
| per-window flag rate | `0.0561` | `0.05` (nominal) |
| campaigns with `any_drift == True` | **`0.385`** | — |
| windows crossing lower / upper limit | `97` / `116` | — |

The statistic is sound; the aggregate is not. `any_drift` is a maximum over nineteen
windowed tests with no multiplicity correction, so a loop keyed on it acts on a false
alarm in two clean campaigns out of five, while presenting the window-level declared
rate.

And both limits are crossed in comparable numbers while `DriftReport` records **which**
nowhere. That matters because the two require **opposite** responses: an upward trip means
the innovations exceed what the model's covariance predicts — the model is wrong, so do
not act on its posterior; a downward trip means they fall short — the covariance is
overstated, so the posterior is *more* informative than it claims and buying information
to reduce it buys nothing. ADR-056's gate needs the distinction and must read
`windowed_mean` against the limits by hand to get it. Filed as **E-49**.

### The action set, and the action no item hosts

Three of the loop's four actions are declared: *set control* and *derate* are
restrictions of item 2's control space; *commission a measurement* draws on item 5's
observation suite. **Retire is declared nowhere.** It is Core §3.9's "declare out of
scope" reached during operation, and Core §4's seven items are exhaustively about the
chain's *content* — state, control, erasure, readouts, observations, invariants, scale —
with no slot for a stopping rule.

The near-miss is item 6, and taking it would be a mistake: an invariant is a conservation
statement the chain satisfies, not a decision about when to stop believing it. Siting the
criterion there would let a domain claim an end-of-life declaration by listing a
conservation law.

That the absence bites rather than being tidy is measurable: contrast's declared operator
drives its own terminal voltage **negative** within forty intervals of ordinary cycling,
and nothing declared bounds it — no validity range, no readout specification limit, no
violated invariant, and item 2's admissible set is declared in words rather than numbers.
Filed as **E-52**, the sixth missing interface category.

### One more thing the trace surfaced, and it is domain thinness rather than a framework defect

Contrast declares three observation modalities; the state schema supports **two**
(`voltage` via `potential`, and `terminal current`, which is a control and known
exactly). *Surface temperature* is declarable and not evaluable, so a "commission the
temperature sensor" action can be named and cannot be costed. The candidate-measurement
pool is smaller than the declaration. Recorded in ADR-056 as a caveat on item 5, **not**
filed as a ledger entry: it is contrast's own declaration overreaching its schema, which
is a repository fact.

Related, and also not a ledger entry: contrast's one evaluable modality has **zero
Jacobian** onto its declared Class-B target. `potential` is a function of lithium
inventory loss and interface resistance; `dendrite_risk` is a function of concentration
overpotential and SEI thickness. The sets are disjoint. That is Core §7.2's declared
"genuinely poor observation suite" taken to its logical end, and it forced the dry-run's
planted defect to modulate two rates rather than one.

## 3. §5.2 — The statistic dry-run

**Two arms**, one generator with one switch (ADR-057). Arm **N**: the declared state is
sufficient; only observation noise. Arm **I**: a scalar latent per cell, **outside**
`CONTRAST_SCHEMA`, scales `porosity_rate` and `resistance_rate` — so Axiom S fails with
respect to the declared schema. Observation noise identical in both: that is the
"exploration noise" the statistic must not mistake for insufficiency.

**The first design was wrong and the correction is worth recording.** The planted defect
was initially a porosity-dependent SEI rate — an *operator* error. Porosity is in the
declared schema, so matching on the full declared state still determines the future,
Axiom S still holds, and the sufficiency deficit is correctly **zero**. It would have made
candidate 1 look inert for a reason that had nothing to do with candidate 1. *"The model
is insufficient"* names two different failures and Spec §1's machinery addresses exactly
one of them.

**Axis:** campaign depth, 6 → 24 intervals. **Axis signal:** the planted insufficiency
moves the observable by `4.53` → `18.13` noise-widths. Non-inert, and growing four-fold.

### Results — the growth ratio, not the separation

Separation is `|mean(Arm I) − mean(Arm N)| / sd(Arm N)`: null-arm sigma units, the only
unit the three candidates share. 40 replicates per arm per depth.

| candidate | separation near → far | **growth** | own magnitude near → far | baseline sep. far | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `deficit` | ∞ → ∞ | **nan** | `3.568e-05` → `5.988e-06` (**×0.168**) | `15.812` | `SEPARATION_UNBOUNDED` |
| `innovation_bias` | `5.155` → `35.432` | **`6.874`** | `3.648` → `20.55` (×5.633) | `15.812` | **`DISCRIMINATING`** |
| `parameter_spread` | `0.982` → `2.849` | `2.902` | `2.444` → `3.345` (×1.369) | `15.812` | `ADVERSE` |

**Selected: `innovation_bias`** — the **signed** mean innovation over a window,
normalised by its own predicted standard error. It passes all three of E-41's criteria in
order and beats the Spec §9.3 tabular baseline by `2.24×` at the far depth.

Note what it is *not*: it is close to the CUSUM variant ADR-026 **rejected** as the
default drift monitor, and it is not the NIS chi-squared statistic the repository
implements. NIS is squared and sign-blind, and a sequence biased by exactly the amount
the model's own covariance predicts passes it. The dry-run's job was to find out which
functional of the innovation sequence an SDL claim needs, and the answer is not the one
already built.

### Candidate 1: two findings pulling opposite ways

The sufficiency deficit is **exactly zero in every null-arm replicate** at both depths —
Spec §1.2's estimator is clamped at zero and a sufficient state leaves nothing above the
clamp. So `sd(null) = 0`, the separation is unbounded, and **E-41's ratio criterion cannot
be evaluated at all**, precisely *because* the statistic detects perfectly. None of
`HoldOutVerdict`'s three failures describes this: the axis is not inert, the models are
not parallel, the candidate is not adverse. And a `nan` silently passes a `<` comparison,
so an implementation following E-41's proposed wording literally would record the
unevaluable case as a **pass** — which this implementation did, on the first run, until
the check was reordered. Filed as **E-50**.

On the one well-defined reading, the deficit **falls** by `5.96×` between the near and far
depth while the insufficiency it is looking for *grew* `4×`. The mechanism is not subtle:
matching is performed on the declared state, and the longer a static latent has been
acting, the more of its value is encoded *in* that state — so units matched late must have
near-equal latents, and their responses agree. The matching procedure launders the missing
variable into the matching criterion.

The practitioner consequence is a false negative in the direction that matters: a campaign
run late in a process, which is when accumulated degradation makes the question urgent,
concludes the state is sufficient. Nothing in Spec §1.2, §1.7 or §8 warns that the
estimator's sensitivity depends on the matching point, and Spec §8's power analysis sizes
`n` as though it did not. Filed as **E-51**.

### Candidate 3: an honest negative

`parameter_spread` diverges (`2.902`) and a gradient-boosted tree separates the arms
better at the far depth (`15.812` vs `2.849`), so it buys nothing. The reason is
structural: only two of the declared operator's rates are identifiable from the one
evaluable modality, and the rate that reaches the *target* — `porosity_rate` — is not
among them. So the statistic is fitted on the half of the defect the observation suite can
see and evaluated on the observable, never on the target.

This is **not** a refutation of ADR-055's parameter triage, which is defined over a
declared constitutive form with named parameters and an out-of-envelope influence
evaluation. It is a finding about this domain: the reduced form contrast can support is
not the statistic ADR-055 specifies. §5.3 measures the real thing on the domain that has
one.

### On the baseline

The brief asked for a GP baseline. This repository has none and one was **not added** —
ADR-039 built gradient-boosted trees and ridge regression in numpy because that is what
Spec §9.3 requires, and a new pinned dependency would make this dry-run's baseline arm
incomparable with M10.2's and M11.5's, the only baseline numbers in the repository. The
substitution is recorded rather than made silently.

## 4. §5.3 — E-47 measured, and narrowed

The declared form is M11.5's contestant 2 — Kocks–Mecking with temperature-dependent
recovery, `θ = (k₁, k₂₀, p)` — refitted 300 times on the same in-envelope design of
experiment with 2% relative observation noise. Every training strain sits at or below the
generator's recrystallisation onset, so the **mechanism set is complete by construction**:
M11's failure mode is absent, not assumed absent.

**Declared metric** (CLAUDE.md invariant 1, and load-bearing here because an angle in
parameter space depends on the scaling): fractional units `θ̃ᵢ = θᵢ/θᵢ*`. Deliberately
*not* the invariant-1 aleatoric default, which here would be the across-seed standard
deviation of the very covariance being measured — it would force the covariance's diagonal
to unity and destroy the anisotropy the measurement exists to find.

### Preconditions hold

| quantity | measured |
|---|---|
| fitted `k₁` / `k₂₀` / `p` | `7.9954` / `1.9983` / `1.5020` (true `8.0` / `2.0` / `1.5`) |
| fractional bias, worst parameter | `1.34e-03` |
| in-envelope relative residual | `0.00280` against `0.02` injected noise |

### Premise confirmed: it is a ridge

Eigenvalues of the fractional-unit covariance: `4.465e-04`, `1.223e-04`, `1.022e-06`.
**Condition number `436.7`.** Stable across three noise levels spanning 1–5% and across
seeds (`397`–`439`), because the geometry is set by the design of experiment and noise only
scales the cloud.

### Consequence narrowed: the ridge rotates, and never aligns

`alignment(γ) = |cos∠(v₁, e(γ))|`, where `v₁` is the least-constrained direction and
`e(γ)` the direction the prediction at accumulated strain `γ` is most sensitive to:

| γ | 1.0 | 1.5 | 2.0 | 3.0 | 4.0 | 6.0 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| alignment | `0.0436` | `0.0895` | `0.1266` | `0.1789` | `0.2106` | `0.2402` |

**Growth `5.51×`** across the reach — monotone, and E-41's divergence reading appearing on
a second axis. The in-envelope value is low almost by construction: `v₁` is nearly the
null direction of the in-envelope Jacobian, which is *why* the fit residual is excellent.

**But `|cos| = 0.2402` at six times the envelope edge is nearer orthogonal than parallel.**
The ridge leans toward the extrapolation-sensitive direction; it does not point along it.
E-47's original wording said *precisely along the direction the extrapolation cares
about*, and that is the part the measurement removes.

### What it costs

| quantity | measured |
|---|---|
| response relative spread, in-envelope | `0.00350` |
| response relative spread at γ=6 | `0.01331` (**×3.80**) |
| far spread / in-envelope fit residual | **`4.75×`** |
| saturation `(k₁/k₂₀)²` relative sd | `0.01127` |

So a practitioner reading the fit residual as the extrapolated prediction's uncertainty is
wrong by a factor of about five, and **nothing in the framework reports the factor** —
which is the surviving gap. What is not supported is "arbitrary": the spread is 1.3% of
the response.

### The "report clean" bullet: refuted

Checked against the **unwindowed** `KOCKS_MECKING`, which declares bounds on strain rate,
temperature and stored density and **none on accumulated strain** — the form E-47's bullet
requires, and the one the source actually establishes; the windowed sibling exists only
because M11.5 needed the strain bound explicit (E-40).

Only **58.3%** of far-strain queries are in-window. The hardest-binding bound is
`stored_density`, against its declared ceiling of `20.0`, because the saturation level the
form predicts passes that ceiling at the cool end of the temperature window.

So the framework is **not silent**. It fires for a substantial minority — and it fires for
the wrong reason, since a saturation ceiling is a statement about a physical regime and
not about whether the data pinned the parameters. "Partial coverage, for an unrelated
reason" is weaker than "silent" and it is what the measurement supports. Reported per
query as a fraction, across the whole declared temperature window; no sub-range was
selected to make the bullet true.

### What is still untested in E-47

The bullet asserting that Spec §3.3's *state-direction* triage reports a clean dangerous
set on the same chain — measuring it needs a `ConformanceInputs` wired for a chain built
for another purpose — and whether a parameter *danger score* (influence × uncertainty,
rather than the spread and orientation measured here) behaves as ADR-055 designs it to.
What was measured is the geometry the design rests on, not the diagnostic itself. E-47's
own confidence field now says so.

## 5. What Part 5(1) changes for what follows

- **Part 5(2)** — the third decision kind — inherits an action set whose fourth member has
  no declared home (E-52) and a diagnostic set of which one is unavailable on contrast and
  one fires arbitrarily. The design decision it must make first is whether the third kind
  is keyed on any *categorical* framework output at all.
- **Part 6** — its discriminating statistic is selected (`innovation_bias`), and E-50 says
  the vacuity gate it must pass through needs a fourth outcome before it can be applied to
  a statistic. Thresholds are still unfixed and belong in Part 6's own earlier commit.
- **The arity redesign** (E-46's consumer) now has a sixth missing category to weigh
  (E-52). §1's finding 5 reads "six independent gaps of the same shape".
