## 1 — The blast radius

### 1a. What consumes the label: measured, and the answer is *nothing numeric*

The concern was that downstream machinery might require a total labelling, pricing
abstention. **It does not.** In this repository the observed/inferred label feeds no
computation:

| Consumer | Reads the label? | Decided by |
|---|---|---|
| `value_of_information` (Spec §3.4) | **No** | prior + Gramian, via Woodbury |
| `best_placement` (Spec §3.4) | **No** | `ΔV_c/cost` over candidates |
| `worst_case_over_window` (Spec §3.6) | **No** | total danger score |
| `variance_term` (Spec §1.3) | **No** | sum of all danger scores |
| `dangerous_set()` — "the actionable output", "the sensing investment case" | **No** | the **influence/uncertainty median split** (E-48's *first* half), not the share |
| Spec §9.1's OMI-1 line item `observability_triage_with_dangerous_set_declared` | **No** | `triage_result is not None` — that a triage was *reported*, not what it says |
| `inferred_set()` | **Yes** — the only consumer | the share threshold |

Nothing in `src/` calls `inferred_set()`. Checked structurally rather than by reading source:
the same chain's numeric outputs are **identical across three window values that change every
label**.

**So the code-side blast radius is: the `Triage` enum, `danger_triage`'s labelling branch,
`inferred_set()`, and three oracle tests that assert labels** (`test_known_latent_trajectory`,
`test_observability_triage`, `test_known_empty_slot`). No numeric result in this repository
moves under any of the three options.

**On the buy-sensing row, one correction to the premise.** The brief says "inferred"
underwrites the buy-sensing row in §11. It does not: that row is underwritten by
**`DANGEROUS`** — influential *and* unidentifiable — which is decided by the median split
(E-48's first half). The observed/inferred split is orthogonal to it. So the intervention
table survives all three options unchanged, and the buy-sensing row's real exposure is to
E-48's *other* degeneracy, which ADR-061 does not address.

### 1c. The papers — a publication consequence, stated plainly

**Neither paper is in this repository**, so this is what the argument requires, not a diff.

Core §3.8 and Spec §3.3 both make *inferred* the framework's central differentiator against a
tabular baseline, and both state it as a **categorical count** — how many directions a tabular
model could not recover. **No option preserves that form.** Under option 2 the membership
depends on a declared `ρ`; under abstention some directions carry no label.

The claim must become a **measured quantity**: *"this direction's information accrues
predominantly downstream, by a dominance ratio of X"* — falsifiable and reportable, where a
count of labelled directions is neither.

**The conclusion survives; the form of its statement does not.** On both implemented domains
option 2 at `ρ = 1.5` reproduces the substantive reading — contrast `inferred` at all twelve
interior readings, flagship `observed` wherever its erasure leaves only near-diagonal
information — matching the current criterion at **eleven of contrast's twelve** readings. The
single reading that changes is the arbitrary flip. So a paper does not lose its argument; it
has to restate it as a measurement and quote a ratio instead of a count.

## 2 — The chosen option

**Accepted: option 2 with option 3's abstention, and the brief's reading that abstention is
the more important half.** ADR-061 carries the decision. The measured fork:

| | option 1 | option 2 | option 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| contrast, all interior indices | `observed`, **including at share `1/12`** | ratio `1.0` everywhere, spread `< 10⁻⁶` | `unresolved` at `ρ = 1` |
| flagship, declared chain | decisive | ratio only `0` or `inf` | never fires |
| distance from the criterion on contrast | n/a | **`0.5`** at `ρ = 1.5` | `0`, and abstains |
| index dependence | none | **none — the ladder is gone** | n/a |

**Option 1 rejected on measurement.** It labels every contrast direction `observed`, including
where 92% of the information accrues downstream. Spec's word *dominates* would carry no
content. It is the cheapest option — no new declared number — and that is not enough.

**Option 2's real advantage is the margin, not parameter elimination.** `ρ` is declared and it
changes the answer: `unresolved` at `ρ = 1`, `inferred` at `ρ = 1.5`. What changes is that the
criterion is approached by `0.5` instead of by `1.4×10⁻¹¹`, and that the ladder disappears
because comparing two equal contributions has no index dependence where summing over a window
and dividing by a total does.

**Abstention is the more important half, and the measurement shows why.** At `ρ = 1` — the
literal reading of *dominates* — contrast's ratio of exactly `1.0` sits on the criterion, and
abstention reports *"the near-diagonal and downstream maxima are equal to eleven figures;
neither label is warranted"*. That is a fact about the chain. Forcing a label there reports a
rounding error as a physical finding.

**The abstention's price, which is not the one feared.** Nothing numeric consumes the label
(§1a), so the code cost is three oracle tests. The real cost is in the conformance ladder:
Spec §9.1's OMI-1 item checks that a triage was *reported*, so a domain abstaining on every
direction would satisfy OMI-1 while establishing nothing — §4's satisfiable-without-the-property
shape, introduced by the fix meant to make the diagnostic honest. **So the decision carries a
reporting obligation**: the **abstained fraction** must be reported, and Spec §9.1's item must
require it. Without that clause the answer would be worse than the problem.

## 3 — The `near_diagonal_window` disposition

**It remains a free parameter and must be declared per domain with a justification. Option 2
does not eliminate it.** Measured, same chain, same indices, same criterion:

| window | dominance ratio | verdict at `ρ = 1.5` |
|---|---|---|
| `0` | `~10⁻³³` | `inferred` |
| `1` | `1.0` | `inferred` |
| `2` | `1.0` and `inf` | `inferred` **and `observed`** |

Reported as the answer to the partial-repair question, because it is the one that would have
been easy to get wrong: fixing the threshold and leaving the window free repairs one of two
compounding conventions and reports it as both.

**What does improve is the character of the dependence.** Under the share, window and threshold
compounded into a label decided at machine precision. Under option 2 each window gives a
*decisive* reading (`~0`, `1`, `inf`), so the window becomes a visible modelling choice a
reader can disagree with rather than a hidden tie-breaker.

**One tempting elimination, rejected.** Fixing `w = 0` would make the "single near-diagonal
term" literally the term at `k`, and contrast then reads a decisive `inferred`. Rejected: it
makes `observed` unreachable for any chain whose readout at `k` has no sensitivity to the
direction in question — contrast's case at every index — which is a property of the *readout*,
not of the information structure. Choosing the window to make one domain's answer clean is
adjusting a criterion to fit a case.

## 4 — The ladder disposition

**The brief's suspicion is right in substance and needs one distinction to be exact.** Both
halves are measured.

**The ladder itself is an artefact, and the fix removes it.** The `1/(D−k)` walk came from
summing over a window and dividing by a total. The dominance ratio is `1.0` at every contrast
index, spread below `10⁻⁶`. Gone.

**The degeneracy beneath it is structural and every fix inherits it.** Filed as **E-54**, a
separate entry, and it is a finding about **Core §3.9's error-control dichotomy** rather than
about the diagnostic:

| class | chain structure | what "which terms supply the information" answers | measured |
|---|---|---|---|
| **condition (b)** — no erasure | every downstream observation contributes the **same** quadratic form | *all of them, equally* — no answer exists | contrast: share ladder `1/(D−k)`; dominance ratio **one value** |
| **condition (a)** — erasure declared | sensitivity survives or is annihilated | *exactly one* — decided before it is asked | flagship, flagship_constitutive: shares exactly `0`/`1`; ratio only `0` or `inf` |

So Core §3.8's distinction is **vacuous** on one class and **trivial** on the other, and
informative only where sensitivity decays *without vanishing*. **Neither implemented domain
does that** — and which extreme a chain lands at is predicted by its **erasure declaration**,
which held as a prediction on the third chain rather than as a fit.

**And it bears on the central argument.** On a condition-(b) chain *every* direction's
information accrues through the chain — which sounds like the strongest case for the framework
and is actually the case where the diagnostic cannot discriminate, because there is no contrast
to draw. The claim survives; the diagnostic offered as its evidence does not discriminate where
the claim is most plausible.

E-54 is **Medium** on the strongest form — that the interior case is *rare* rather than merely
absent here. Two domains chosen to invert each other on this axis are not a sample. What would
settle it is a chain with decaying-but-nonzero sensitivity (a diffusive or relaxing operator
without a rank-deficient Jacobian). None exists here, and building one is the natural next
measurement rather than something E-54 claims.
