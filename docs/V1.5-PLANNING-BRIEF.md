## Part 1 — the purpose extension

v1.3's stated purpose is representing and learning PSR linkages. v1.5 extends
it: **the framework's purpose is deciding what to do next under uncertainty.**
Prediction becomes a means; the diagnostics become the product.

This is a scope change, not a feature, so it must be declared rather than
allowed to drift.

Write the ADR that:

- states the extended purpose and what it demotes — Class B machinery and
  erasure analysis become supporting results; the danger-score triage, the
  intervention table (`V1.4-EDITS.md` §11) and the refusal criterion become
  the spine;
- states what happens to existing conformance results, which were claimed
  against the old purpose. My recommendation, to accept or argue against:
  they remain valid and citable as v1.3-purpose results, and v1.5 conformance
  is a separate claim. Do not silently reinterpret them;
- proposes the Core §1.1 and abstract rewording, without applying it.

**Gate.** The ADR, and a list of every Core/Spec section whose text the
extension makes inaccurate.

## Part 2 — the declared-domain construction (do this before Part 3)

E-22 established that `ν` is mis-typed: it needs its own domain `𝒳_ν`, which
may be the whole body, a subdomain, or a single global point. The composition
decomposition of Part 3 needs exactly the same thing for `c̄`.

**These are one construction with two consumers.** Design it once.

Requirements:

- a declared domain object: global point, declared regions, or a field over a
  declared dimension set;
- a declared **coupling direction** — E-22's second proposed edit:
  *determined-by* the point states (eliminable, instantaneous),
  *depleted-by* them under a conservation law (non-eliminable, carries its
  own dynamics), or intermediate. A corrected spatial type with the wrong
  coupling dynamics is no better than the current under-declaration;
- a **declared tracked-dimension set** — through-thickness only, in-plane
  only, full 3-D, or none — with a **required justification field**. This is
  Spec §4.4's dimensional-reduction argument arriving on a different axis:
  for planar product, through-thickness is where gradients live and in-plane
  is effectively uniform, so a 1-D profile captures the real behaviour at a
  fraction of a 3-D field's cost. The justification is what stops this
  becoming an unexamined default the way the point-valued state did.

**Scope discipline.** Declare the full construction; implement the global-point
and single-region cases. Multi-region raises `NotSpecified` citing Core §2.5.
Do not resolve §2.5 in v1.5.

**Gate.** The ADR, and an explicit statement of which E-22 sub-claims this
closes and which remain open.

## Part 3 — composition

### 3.1 The decomposition

$$c(x) = \\bar{c} + \\delta c(x)$$

- `c̄` — the mean over a declared domain (Part 2). Constant along the chain by
  definition; that constancy is checkable and a violation means the
  declaration is wrong. It indexes the operator family.
- `δc(x)` — the fluctuation field. Lives in whichever slot its length scale
  puts it in: sub-resolution clustering in `z`, resolved bands in `m`,
  boundary segregation in `Γ`. Ordinary state, evolving under ordinary
  operators.

The split is set by the declared projection scale, which is itself a function
of the characterisation suite (`PHYSICS-ADEQUACY.md` §3.3). Operator reuse
between two implementations is only valid if their splits match, and nothing
currently detects a mismatch.

**Implement:** `c̄` plus sub-resolution `δc` in `z`. Resolved bands stay
declarable and unimplemented.

### 3.2 Role declaration

Chemistry's role is declared per species per region, not fixed:

| Role | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Parameter** | fixed for the chain; indexes the operator family |
| **Control** | set by the experimenter; lives in `𝒰_adm` |
| **State** | evolves under some operator |

The same quantity takes different roles in different chains — bulk carbon is
a parameter in a hot-stamping chain and a control in a discovery campaign.
The interface must express both.

### 3.3 The metric

`c̄` is declared over **physics descriptors** — carbon equivalent, Ms,
hardenability index, stacking-fault energy, valence electron count — with raw
fractions declared as the underlying space.

The reason is checkability, not convenience: *"this operator depends on
chemistry only through carbon equivalent"* is a falsifiable claim, testable by
holding out a composition that varies other elements at fixed CE. Raw
fractions give a meaningless metric; a learned embedding gives no transfer
guarantee and fails silently across a phase boundary.

Note the simplex constraint — components sum to one — needs the same hard
structural treatment phase fractions already get.

### 3.4 Two consequences to design for

**A third inverse problem.** v1.3 has the control inverse (find `u`) and the
structure inverse (find `s`). `π` gives the **composition inverse** (find the
chemistry) — which is what materials discovery is, and it now falls out of the
formalism rather than being bolted on.

**Validity ranges become functions of composition.** Ms is not universal;
Koistinen–Marburger's `α` is not universal. So a declared form's validity
interval is a function over composition space, not a pair of numbers. This is
a genuine extension of ADR-043's mechanism, not a reuse. It yields the
**regime-boundary check** — *does the proposed chemistry still support my
declared mechanism set?* — which crossing a phase boundary can violate. That
is mechanism incompleteness (M11's finding) arriving on the composition axis.

**Gate.** ADRs for the decomposition, role declaration, descriptor metric,
composition inverse, and composition-dependent validity. Report which parts of
Core §4's interface change.

## Part 4 — parameter equifinality

A distinct failure from M11's. The form is complete; the parameters are
unidentifiable along the axis being extrapolated. Fit Kocks–Mecking's `k₁` and
`k₂` in-envelope and many pairs fit equally well — implying different
saturation levels, which is exactly what extrapolation approaches. Nothing in
the current framework would flag it.

Design the extension of the observability triage from **state directions** to
**declared-form parameters**: same Fisher-information construction, same
danger score, different object. It answers *is my declared physics
identifiable along the axis I am extrapolating?*

For a self-driving lab this is an experiment-selection signal, not a warning —
it names which measurement would disambiguate the parameters.

**Gate.** The ADR. State plainly whether this reuses the §3 machinery or needs
a parallel construction; if parallel, justify it.

## Part 5 — the SDL instantiation

A **sibling** domain, joining the flagship rather than replacing it. Flagship
catches up on the composition axis separately (Part 6).

Chosen because it exercises what the framework's new purpose rests on and the
existing domains do not: a controllable composition axis, sparse expensive
measurements, and a real decision at each step.

Declare the seven interface items plus the v1.5 additions. Design only.

**Gate.** The declaration, machine-diffable against flagship and contrast, and

## Part 6 — the experiment, and the statistic it needs

The pre-registered claim:

> **The framework identifies when a campaign is failing due to model
> insufficiency rather than exploration noise; a GP-based acquisition cannot.**

This is the narrower, defensible claim — deliberately not the stronger
"reaches target in fewer runs," which GP-BO is very good at and which would
likely refute.

**The claim is not yet operational, and that is what Part 6 must fix.** It
needs a **discriminating statistic**: something computed per campaign step
that separates model insufficiency from exploration noise. Candidates in
existing machinery — a sufficiency deficit trending upward while the
acquisition continues proposing; innovations biased rather than white;
parameter danger scores rising along the proposed direction. Which one, and
its pre-registered threshold, must be decided **in planning**.

Design, do not run:

- the statistic, with its null (noise) and alternative (insufficiency);
- the construction under which the ground truth is known — a campaign where
  insufficiency is introduced deliberately at a known step;
- **the vacuity gate from E-41 applied to this experiment.** Verify that the
  statistic and the GP baseline actually diverge along the axis being tested,
  and that the disagreement *grows*. M11.4 satisfied every stated requirement
  and measured nothing; the precondition exists to stop that recurring;
- thresholds, to be committed in a separate earlier commit than the sweep.

**Gate.** The experiment design and the pre-registration plan. Report whether
the vacuity gate passes on the proposed axis **before** any thresholds are
fixed.
