# FIGURE-SOURCES.md — every result that could become a figure, and the caveat that must travel with it

For writing from, not for the repository's own use. Each row below is a result a
paper might turn into a figure or table, with **what it shows**, the **committed
source** of its numbers, the **command** that regenerates them, and — where it
applies — the **caveat that must appear in the caption**. A figure lifted without
its caveat is how a careful build produces a careless paper; the caveat column is
the reason this document exists.

Runtimes and expected outputs are in `REPRODUCE.md`. Every figure's numbers are
regenerated by a committed script or asserted by a named test; none requires new
analysis code.

---

## The M11 headline results

### F1 — The M11.5 contestant table (the central refutation)

- **Shows.** On a gated accumulated-strain hold-out, every declared-form contestant
  is worse than a free-form operator; a tabular baseline is best of six. Held-out
  RMSE pooled over held-out strains: free-form 5.06, correct-form 8.10, 3a 69.55,
  3b 7.56, Ridge 24.31, GBT 3.27.
- **Source.** `docs/M11.5-EXTRAPOLATION.md` §1 (narrative); observation
  keys under `m11_5_*` in `build/observations.json`.
- **Regenerate.** `python scripts/run_m11_5_extrapolation.py` (~22 s);
  `pytest tests/test_m11_5_extrapolation.py -q` for the pass/fail form.
- **CAVEAT — contestant 2 is a ceiling, not a headline.** The correct form with
  fitted parameters recovers the generator's parameters exactly (noiseless training
  region), so its 8.10 is the *irreducible* cost of not knowing the withheld term.
  ADR-045 fixed it as a calibration reference before the run; reporting it as a
  competitor overstates the comparison. The claim is `gap(3,1)` and `gap(3,4)`, not
  `gap(2,·)`.

### F2 — The misspecification asymmetry

- **Shows.** A missing *dependence* costs almost nothing (3b, 7.56); a missing
  *mechanism* is catastrophic (3a, 69.55, growing 11.5 → 30.4 → 56.2 → 123.0 with
  accumulated strain because it cannot saturate). Gap between the two arms: +61.98.
- **Source.** `docs/M11.5-EXTRAPOLATION.md` §3; `docs/M11-RECORD.md` §5.
- **Regenerate.** `python scripts/run_m11_5_extrapolation.py` (the "ROBUSTNESS BY
  MISSPECIFICATION KIND" block and the per-strain curves);
  `pytest tests/test_m11_5_extrapolation.py::test_a_missing_mechanism_is_far_worse_than_a_missing_dependence -q`.
- **CAVEAT — the +61.98 is measured against the declared minimum effect 0.63, a
  different comparison from the −64.49 free-form-vs-3a gap (against τ = 0.331).**
  Do not quote +61.98 against 0.32; that crosses two comparisons. The asymmetry is
  the paper's practitioner claim; the −64.49 is the refutation claim. Same
  experiment, different thresholds.

### F3 — The signed-bias error-cancellation diagnostic (E-42)

- **Shows.** The declared forms do not lose through ignorance of their own physics.
  Against a truth with the withheld term removed the ranking inverts: correct form
  0.0000 (first), winner GBT 8.52 (fourth). The winner wins by a −7.49 bias that
  nearly cancels the withheld term's −6.56.
- **Source.** `docs/M11.5-EXTRAPOLATION.md` §2; observation
  `m11_5_ranking_inverts_between_the_two_truths`; `docs/V1.4-EDITS.md` E-42.
- **Regenerate.** `python scripts/run_m11_5_extrapolation.py` (the "DIAGNOSTIC"
  block); `pytest tests/test_m11_5_extrapolation.py::test_the_declared_forms_lose_by_cancellation_not_by_ignorance -q`.
- **CAVEAT — this does NOT un-refute the claim, and the diagnostic is unavailable on
  field data.** The registered criterion was full-truth RMSE; the declared forms
  lost on it. The withheld-free decomposition needs a generator you can modify, so
  on real data a win by cancellation and a win by understanding are
  indistinguishable. Present F3 as a finding *about the criterion* (E-42), never as
  a rescue of the declared forms.

### F4 — The M11.4 vacuous-axis diagnostic (E-39/E-41)

- **Shows.** A hold-out can satisfy every Spec §9.3 requirement and still be unable
  to discriminate. On M11.4's strain-rate axis every contestant's held-out error is
  the withheld term itself; against a drag-free truth the free-form arm (0.077) beats
  the correct declared form (0.339) because the form has no rate dependence. The
  discrimination gate returns INERT_AXIS here (axis signal 0.000, divergence 1.05)
  and DISCRIMINATING on M11.5's axis (divergence 14.57 against a bar of 2.0).
- **Source.** `docs/M11.4-EXTRAPOLATION.md` §2; observations `m11_4_axis_discrimination`,
  `m11_4_axis_fails_all_three_criteria`, `m11_5_axis_discrimination`;
  `docs/V1.4-EDITS.md` E-39, E-41.
- **Regenerate.** `pytest tests/test_holdout_discrimination.py -q` (~50 s, the fast
  path); `python scripts/run_m11_4_extrapolation.py` (~4 min, the full diagnostic).
- **CAVEAT — M11.4's null is *uninformative*, not negative.** Do not present M11.4's
  numbers as evidence about declared forms; the milestone's result is the diagnosis
  of why the comparison was vacuous. And E-41 supersedes E-39's proposed wording:
  "materially different predictions" is inoperable (the two axes show the same
  absolute disagreement); the criterion is divergence, not difference.

## The pre-M11 diagnostic machinery (M2–M6)

### F5 — The erasure spectrum on a real chain stage (E-19)

- **Shows.** Flagship's HEATING_AND_SOAK stage is **full rank (7) at the default
  tolerance** yet carries two near-erased directions visible only in the singular
  spectrum (two values near e⁻⁵ ≈ 0.0067, the rest ≥ 0.74). A rank check reports
  "no erasure"; the spectrum shows otherwise.
- **Source.** observations `rank`, `spectrum`, `tol` in
  `tests/test_flagship_real_erasure.py`; `docs/V1.4-EDITS.md` E-19; ADR-017 (the
  tolerance choice).
- **Regenerate.** `pytest tests/test_flagship_real_erasure.py -q`.
- **CAVEAT — this is the E-19 point: a rank/threshold diagnostic is blind to a
  near-erasure that the spectrum makes obvious.** The figure argues for reporting
  the spectrum, not a rank verdict. Do not caption it "no erasure at HEATING_AND_SOAK"
  — that is precisely the blind reading E-19 corrects. The tolerance is metric-
  dependent (ADR-017); state it.

### F6 — The Class B ranking inversion

- **Shows.** Material A is weaker at a thick process zone, B weaker at a thin one —
  the failure-probability ranking inverts with geometry, driven by B's dimensional
  reduction (N_eff falls for B between thick and thin, is flat for A), not by
  severity alone.
- **Source.** observations `p_fail_a_thick/b_thick/a_thin/b_thin`,
  `difference_thick/thin`, `n_eff_*` in
  `tests/oracles/test_known_ranking_inversion.py`.
- **Regenerate.** `pytest tests/oracles/test_known_ranking_inversion.py -q`.
- **CAVEAT — this is FORMULA SELF-CONSISTENCY, not an emergent effect (E-14).** The
  oracle recovers a ranking inversion that the weakest-link construction puts in by
  hand; it validates that the estimator reads the construction correctly, and does
  **not** validate Class B's dimensional-reduction proposition as physics. The file
  carries a permanently-skipped test
  (`test_ranking_inversion_is_not_empirically_validated_as_an_emergent_effect`)
  whose presence is the record of exactly this. A caption claiming an emergent or
  measured effect misrepresents it.

### F7 — The tail-index transfer

- **Shows.** A driver tail index `ξ_a` maps through a known map exponent `β` to a
  response tail index `ξ_D = β·ξ_a`; the estimator recovers `ξ_D` within tolerance,
  and `ξ_D` is smaller for `β = 0.5` than for `β = 1.0`.
- **Source.** observations `xi_a_hat`, `xi_d_hat`, `xi_d_true`,
  `xi_d_small_beta`, `xi_d_large_beta` in `tests/oracles/test_known_tail.py`;
  `docs/V1.4-EDITS.md` E-13.
- **Regenerate.** `pytest tests/oracles/test_known_tail.py -q`.
- **CAVEAT — the transfer is degenerate at β ≈ 1 (E-13).** At `β ≈ 1` the transfer
  machinery reduces to an identity map, so a "successful transfer" there is a no-op
  that any implementation passes by construction — it is not evidence the transfer
  logic works. Spec §4.3 gives no guidance for this case; the informative regime is
  `β` away from 1. Caption the β = 0.5 point as the load-bearing one.

### F8 — The observability triage on two domains

- **Shows.** Per-direction classification of state directions into observed /
  inferred / unresolved / dangerous relative to a *declared* target set, on flagship and
  contrast; contrast's poorer observation suite leaves a larger fraction of target
  variance dangerous.
- **Source.** observations `flagship_label_set`, `contrast_label_set`,
  `flagship_influence_median`, `contrast_influence_median`,
  `flagship_unresolved_danger_fraction`, `contrast_unresolved_danger_fraction` in
  `tests/test_domain_triage.py`.
- **Regenerate.** `pytest tests/test_domain_triage.py -q`.
- **CAVEAT — the triage is defined against a DECLARED readout set (invariant 10); it
  is invalid if the target set changes.** State the target set in the caption. Note
  also that `influence_median == 0` collapses two of the four nominal categories
  (OBSERVED_BUT_IRRELEVANT / MARGINALISABLE) on both domains, so the label set seen
  is a subset of {inferred, dangerous, **unresolved**} (ADR-061) — a figure showing "four categories" would
  overstate what these domains exercise.

## The invariance claim

### F9 — Audit-gate invariance across all M11 changes

- **Shows.** Every observation recorded before M11 touched `src/omi/` is
  byte-identical after M11; M11 only added observations (267 → 306, 0 changed, 0
  missing). This is the evidence for composition-over-modification (ADR-042).
- **Source.** baseline `audit/baselines/v13-items7.json` (267 rows, 267 audited `(test, name)` pairs, at `57f7db8`; ADR-069 re-keyed the comparison after `docs/V1.4-EDITS.md`
  E-60 found the prior name-only keying silently dropped 44 of the 267 from comparison, verified zero drift before re-freezing); `docs/DECISIONS.md` ADR-042.
- **Regenerate.** `scripts/check_audit_gate.sh audit/baselines/v13-items7.json`
  (~110 s; runs the full suite then compares).
- **CAVEAT — the invariance is against the `57f7db8` baseline specifically** (the
  last commit before the extension touched `src/omi/`). It certifies the *extension*
  changed nothing pre-existing; it is not a determinism claim (that is a separate
  check, `scripts/check_determinism.sh`) and not a correctness claim about the
  pre-M11 numbers themselves.

## A note on which figures carry the paper

Under the reframing in `docs/M11-RECORD.md`: **F4 (E-39/E-41) and F3 (E-42) are the
most transferable** — they are defects in Spec §9.3, the framework's own protocol
for deciding whether to use itself, and they apply to anyone running a
physics-versus-baseline extrapolation comparison in materials, OMI or not. **F1 and
F2 are the domain result.** F5–F8 are the pre-M11 measurement machinery that
establishes the build measures what it claims to; each is sound within its caveat
and misleading without it.

## Addendum — the observed/inferred figure candidate, after ADR-061

**Do not draw a figure that counts inferred directions.** `docs/V1.4-EDITS.md` E-55 measured
that the superseded criterion's count included directions carrying *no* observational
information — on flagship, the two with the largest danger scores. A bar chart of label
populations would reproduce that inflation in a form no caption can undo.

The figure that is safe, and better: the **dominance ratio per direction** against the declared
factor `ρ`, with the abstention band shaded and the abstained fraction quoted. Source:
`TriageResult.directions[*].dominance_ratio` and `TriageResult.abstained_fraction`. The caveat
that must travel with it is the declared convention — `ρ`, the band and the window — because the
reading moves with all three (E-48).

A second figure candidate the E-53 milestone created and which has no such hazard: the
**turnover curve** from `tests/oracles/test_known_decaying_sensitivity.py` — dominance ratio
against decay rate `λ`, with the closed-form `λ^{−2(w+1)}` overlaid and the three label regions
marked. It shows the criterion discriminating, locates the turnover, and is the evidence that
E-54's strong form was refuted.
