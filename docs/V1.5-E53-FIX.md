# V1.5-E53-FIX.md — the observed/inferred criterion corrected, and E-54 settled

**Live document** (CLAUDE.md §10). Every number is asserted by
`tests/test_domain_triage.py`, `tests/oracles/test_known_decaying_sensitivity.py`, or the
audit-gate run recorded in §4.

**Scope.** The E-53 fix, implemented per ADR-061's settled design, plus the one measurement
that settles E-54's confidence. Part 6 is not begun.

---

## 1 — What was implemented

**`Triage.UNRESOLVED`** — a sixth value. The criterion is now ADR-061's, replacing ADR-020's
share threshold:

| outcome | condition |
|---|---|
| `observed` | `max_{k ≤ j ≤ k+w} q_j > ρ · max_{j > k+w} q_j` |
| `inferred` | the near-diagonal contribution vanishes at the declared tolerance while the direction is identifiable from the total — Spec §3.3's *"only through Σ_{j>k}"* |
| **`unresolved`** | the ratio sits within a declared band of `ρ`, **or the ratio is undefined** because neither side contributes |

**`ObservedInferredConvention`** carries `ρ`, the abstention band, the window and a **required
justification**, validated at construction. **`TriageResult.abstained_fraction`** is a required
output; `unresolved_set()` sits beside `inferred_set()`. `near_diagonal_share` is **retained and
reported** but is no longer the criterion — dropping it would break the audit trail of every
result that quoted it.

**Declared per domain, each with a justification** (not defaults):

| domain | `ρ` | band | window | the justification's load-bearing part |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| flagship | 2.0 | 0.25 | 0 | an erasure makes the ratio structurally `0`/`∞`, so a demanding factor is free — and the justification says the values are cheap *because* the domain is degenerate |
| contrast | 1.5 | 0.25 | 0 | window 0 is load-bearing: at 1 or 2 the near-diagonal set admits observations whose sensitivity is *identical* to the downstream ones, and the label then moves with the window rather than the physics |
| discovery | 2.0 | **0.5** | 0 | the one value chosen against convenience: no chain exists (ADR-060), so no measurement supports a narrow band, and a wide band abstains more often |

`DEFAULT_CONVENTION` exists so that not every call site changed at once — which is what keeps
the audit gate able to show that no number moved — and its own justification string says a
domain should declare its own.

### The substantive correction, which was not the expected one

The interesting change is not the abstention band. It is the **undefined ratio**.

A direction can be classed *identifiable* by the uncertainty median — a tight **prior**, not
data — while carrying **no Gramian information at all**. ADR-020's rule swept those into
`inferred` through its `else` branch. Spec §3.3 requires inferred directions to be identifiable
*from the total*, which they are not.

Measured on flagship, at its own declared query index:

| | superseded criterion | ADR-061 |
|---|---|---|
| `inferred` danger scores | `[7.9999999798, 4.3085686918, 0.0, 0.0]` | `[0.0]` |
| `unresolved` | — | contains **both** nonzero-danger directions |

**Flagship's two largest danger scores were counted as `inferred` — the framework's
differentiator, "what no tabular baseline can recover" — and no observation informs them at
all.** Filed as **E-55**, and it is what makes the publication consequence concrete rather than
theoretical: the count the papers cite was inflated, on this repository's own headline domain,
by directions the chain does not recover either.

### Core and Spec were not edited, and that is deliberate

The milestone's brief asked for Core §3.8 and Spec §3.3 to be reworded. **They are the v1.3
specification under audit**, not repository-owned documents: all 54 ledger entries cite
locations in them, and editing the text would make those citations unverifiable and make this
repository the framework's author rather than its implementer.

The replacement wording is where the repository's conventions put it — E-53's and E-55's
`Proposed wording` fields, which now carry complete pasteable text for both sections. The four
repository-owned documents that restate the criterion were reworded **in place**: `CLAUDE.md`'s
vocabulary table, `docs/ROADMAP.md` M3 and M5, `docs/COVERAGE.md`'s S-3.3 row, and
`docs/FIGURE-SOURCES.md` (with an addendum saying **do not draw a figure that counts inferred
directions**). `docs/REVIEW_PACK.md` is a commit-stamped snapshot and received a **dated
addendum**, not an edit.

**The two numeric ones were retired, not changed, and the distinction is the honest answer to
"did a number move".** Their per-direction danger scores are byte-identical; the *filter*
selecting them changed. Withdrawing the name and reissuing under a new one makes that visible;
reusing the name would have been the one way this change could have moved a number while
appearing not to.

**ADR-062** extends the gate with declared exceptions and two guards, and the guards are in the
ADR because the first version lacked one of them and failed correctly:

- a declared **change** may not be numeric — the mechanical form of the claim this rests on;
- a **retirement** must name a replacement, and the replacement must be present in the run —
  otherwise "retired" is a way to delete an inconvenient observation.

The gate still **FAILS on any undeclared movement**. Nothing was relaxed.

## 3 — E-54 settled: the strong form is refuted, the narrow form is High

E-54 named the missing measurement itself: a chain with **decaying but non-annihilating**
downstream sensitivity. Built as `tests/oracles/known_decaying_sensitivity.py` — a diagonal
linear map with per-step factor `λ`, **full-rank at every `λ > 0`**, so sensitivity decays
without being annihilated. The dominance ratio is `λ^{−2(w+1)}` in closed form, known before
any Gramian is computed.

| `λ` | 0.995 | 0.95 | 0.9 | **0.8** | 0.7 | 0.5 | 0.3 | 0.1 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| measured ratio | 1.010 | 1.108 | 1.235 | **1.563** | 2.041 | 4.000 | 11.11 | 100.0 |
| label | inferred | inferred | inferred | **unresolved** | observed | observed | observed | observed |

Closed form matched to `< 10⁻⁸` at every point. Turnover at `ρ^{−1/(2(w+1))} = 0.8165`, exactly
where predicted. The abstention fires in a neighbourhood of it — the case the band was designed
for, and one neither implemented domain supplies.

**So the criterion discriminates on an intermediate chain, with a locatable turnover.** E-54's
**strong form — "structurally uninformative across the board" — is refuted.** Its **narrow
form** goes to **High**: *the two declared classes of Core §3.9's dichotomy are the degenerate
extremes of Core §3.8's distinction, and the framework says so nowhere.* Measured on four
chains, three real and one constructed, the constructed one predicted in closed form before it
was run.

**One narrowing of E-48 the same measurement forces, recorded because it is honest rather than
because it helps.** The superseded *share* also varies continuously across the sweep
(`0.147 → 0.990`). So the share is **not** uninformative in general — it is degenerate **at the
extremes**, where it becomes a rational ladder or a boolean, and both implemented domains sit
there. ADR-061's case rests on the margin and on Spec's own wording, and E-48 should not be read
as the stronger claim.

**Why this is good news for the framework, stated because the honest reading is not the gloomy
one.** A distinction that discriminates with a locatable turnover against a closed form is a
better instrument than a count of labels ever was. What the framework lacks is not the
diagnostic but a statement of its scope — and both implemented domains happen to sit outside it.

**The last row matters more than it looks.** The measurements that *justified* ADR-061 were
taken under the superseded criterion. Re-pointing them at the new one would have destroyed the
evidence for the change — a repository cannot show why it changed a criterion using only the
criterion it changed to. So the superseded rule is re-expressed locally in those oracle modules
(`superseded_label`), applied to the share `danger_triage` still reports, and clearly labelled
as superseded. The evidence survives the change it caused.

## 5 — What Part 6 inherits

- A criterion with a **declared** convention per domain, an abstention outcome, and a required
  abstained fraction — so a Part 6 claim about what the chain tells you that an instrument
  cannot has a per-direction ratio to cite instead of a count.
- **E-55's constraint on how that claim may be phrased**, which applies to Part 6 as much as to
  the papers: never cite the size of the inferred set.
- E-54 at High in its narrow form, which says the discovery domain — declaring an erasure — sits
  at the *trivial* extreme, so its observed/inferred readings will be structurally decisive and
  uninformative. Part 6 should not rest anything on them.
